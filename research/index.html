BitConcepts · Research

Research, held to evidence.

BitConcepts runs open research programs built to be proven wrong. Every claim carries its falsifier, verdicts are pre-registered before runs, controls are designed to be able to fail, and negative results are published next to positive ones. Each program below has its own page with its live status, verdicts, and archived releases.
