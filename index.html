Where did BitConcepts consulting go?

All BitConcepts consulting and engineering services are now delivered through ElectroHire.

What does BitConcepts do now?

BitConcepts owns and runs the Indus decipherment research program (Glossa-Lab), which tests claims about the Indus script one by one against pre-registered criteria. The script is not deciphered; the program publishes what survives testing and what fails.

Where is the BitConcepts research?

The public record is the Indus Program Tracker, with releases archived on Zenodo (latest: v5.0.0) and the project record on OSF.
